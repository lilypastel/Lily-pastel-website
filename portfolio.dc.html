Peace by Piece

Peace by Piece is an exploration of quiet restoration, the gradual process of finding harmony within fragments. Soft layers of ivory, blush, amber, and warm gold dissolve into one another, creating a luminous field that feels suspended between stillness and movement.

The textured surface carries traces of gestures, marks, and delicate fragments, evoking the way peace is rarely found all at once, but assembled slowly through moments of acceptance, renewal, and presence.

Peace by Piece is a meditation on becoming whole, not through perfection, but through the beauty of every piece that remains.

Aurora

Aurora is an exploration of light in motion, a luminous convergence of color, texture, and transformation. Layers of rose, violet, coral, amber, and turquoise flow across the surface, creating an atmosphere that feels both dreamlike and alive.

Fragments of gold emerge through the shifting fields of color like distant light breaking through clouds, while gestural marks create a sense of movement and rhythm.

Aurora is a celebration of wonder and emergence, a reminder that beauty often reveals itself in the spaces between chaos and harmony.

Kissed by the Sun

Kissed by the Sun is an abstract meditation on light, warmth, and the vitality of nature. Deep blues and luminous turquoise form an expansive atmosphere, while vivid coral, crimson, green, and violet rise through the composition.

Strands of gold move across the surface, catching and reflecting illumination like sunlight breaking through foliage.

Kissed by the Sun evokes the fleeting moment when light transforms everything it touches, a celebration of radiance, movement, and the quiet energy of being illuminated.

Eclectric

Eclectric exists at the intersection of eclecticism and electricity, a vibrant collision of color, texture, and energy. Bold fields of fluorescent pink, luminous yellow, fiery orange, deep indigo, and soft neutrals converge in a composition that feels both spontaneous and deliberate.

Layered gestures, raw edges, and textured passages create a visual rhythm that shifts between structure and freedom.

The title Eclectric captures the spirit of the work: eclectic in its language, electric in its energy.

Rooted

An homage to Amazigh heritage, ancestry & connection.

A tribute to heritage, identity, and the invisible threads that connect us to where we come from.

The intertwined threads represent generations connected through culture, memory, and tradition, carrying the strength of those who came before us.

The earthy tones and tactile textures evoke the land, while the roots extending beyond the canvas suggest that our heritage cannot be contained within a frame.

Under Her Eye

A quiet meditation on presence, perception, and the unseen forces that shape us.

Against a deep, almost nocturnal field of teal, a luminous burst of gold emerges, fragmented yet whole, delicate yet commanding.

Under Her Eye invites the viewer into that suspended moment between being seen and seeing: where vulnerability becomes strength.

Oh Gold

Gold does not simply shine here, it remembers. In Oh Gold, layers of luminous texture hold fragments of darkness, as if something precious has been buried beneath the surface, waiting to be seen.

Beside Under Her Eye, the two works become a quiet conversation: the eye observes, and gold reveals.

Together, they speak of power, femininity, mystery, and the things we carry unseen. Under Her Eye. Oh Gold. Two works, one gaze.

Elemental

Elemental explores the quiet power of nature through form, contrast, and restraint. Organic shapes drift across the surface in earthy tones of sand, terracotta, charcoal, and warm umber.

The interplay of soft curves and grounded forms evokes the fundamental forces of earth and fire, growth and stillness, warmth and shadow.

Elemental is an invitation to return to something instinctive: the beauty of what is raw, grounded, and essential.
